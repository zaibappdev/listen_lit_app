## Short description

Play music on your device and discover Creative Commons tracks from Jamendo.

## Long description

Listen Lit brings your device music and licensed online discovery into one clean player. Browse music stored on your phone, explore Jamendo tracks with artist and license credits, and keep listening with Android notification and lock-screen controls. Choose Light, Dark, or System appearance and control playback from the app, notification shade, or headset buttons.

Online discovery requires a Jamendo API client ID. Track availability and usage rights follow Jamendo's API terms and each track's license. No offline downloads are currently offered.
